Parkwell

Civic tech · mobile · 2023

Parkwell is a parking payment product used by a handful of mid-size cities. I was asked to help with the pay-and-extend flow in the mobile app — a deceptively small surface that carries a lot of error states, location quirks, and “I just need this to work on the sidewalk” energy. I collaborated with the in-house designer and an iOS/Android pair over about three months.

We looked at how people found their zone, how they paid, and what happened when a session was about to expire. A lot of the existing UI assumed you already knew the mental model. I proposed a tighter sequence and a more honest empty/error treatment, then designed the screens in the current app shell so engineering wouldn’t have to rebuild navigation.

Process

  • App review and analytics walkthrough
  • Flows
  • UI design
  • Prototype
  • Dev handoff

Outcome

The city partners saw increased engagement with in-app extensions and fewer support tickets around failed payments. The design is in production. I’d like to do a proper follow-up study; we haven’t scheduled one yet.
