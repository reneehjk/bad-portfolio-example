ParkMingle — municipal parking, but make it dating

problem

(intentionally left blank)

what I shipped

The problem is omitted. We redesigned a municipal parking meter as a dating app. Matches are based on how long you parked. I feel good about this. There are no wireframes here because wireframes are a vibe and vibes cannot be screenshotted. The only artifact is this page.

how I got there

I had leftover pad thai for lunch and the noodles were stuck together which is how I arrived at the information architecture: everything is one long noodle. Users scroll forever. That is the product. Stakeholders asked for a sitemap and I sent a photo of the noodles. Usability test: I asked a stranger at the meter if they were "seeing anyone" and then I left.
fig. a cat. this is not the parking UI.

impact

Impact: it went well. The city did not sue. Downloads were a figure. Matches: anecdotal. I would work with them again if they email me, which they cannot because contact is three clicks away from a page that is not this one.
