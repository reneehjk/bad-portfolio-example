CASE STUDY THREE OR POSSIBLY FOUR

app

The problem is omitted. We redesigned a municipal parking meter as a dating app. Matches are based on how long you parked. I feel good about this. There are no wireframes here because wireframes are a vibe and vibes cannot be screenshotted.
I had leftover pad thai for lunch and the noodles were stuck together which is how I arrived at the information architecture: everything is one long noodle. Users scroll forever. That is the product. Stakeholders asked for a sitemap and I sent a photo of the noodles.
Impact: it went well. The city did not sue. Downloads were a figure. I would work with them again if they email me, which they cannot because contact is three clicks away from a page that is not this one.
