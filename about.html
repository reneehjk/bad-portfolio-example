About

Product designer based in New York, currently looking for my next role.

I'm a product designer with a background in digital media and a focus on user-centric design. I enjoy working on products that help people complete everyday tasks in a more seamless and intuitive way.

After studying design, I completed a UX certificate and began working on digital products across fintech, e-commerce, healthcare, and consumer apps. I collaborate with product managers, engineers, and researchers to bridge the gap between user needs and business goals.

My process typically includes research, ideation, wireframing, prototyping, and testing. I believe great design is about solving real problems and creating experiences that feel simple, clear, and delightful.

When I'm not designing, I love coffee and problem-solving — whether that's a product challenge or a weekend puzzle.
