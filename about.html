Hey, I'm Avery!

Product Designer | UX | UI | Researcher | Creative Thinker · Austin, TX (open to anywhere!)

Ever since I was a kid I loved drawing. I would sit at the kitchen table for hours with a box of crayons and just create, create, create. My mom still has a folder of my early "designs" which were mostly horses and logos for businesses that didn't exist. I think even then I knew I wanted to make things that people would see and feel something.

In middle school I discovered Photoshop tutorials on YouTube and it honestly changed my life. I started making thumbnails for my friends' gaming channels and album covers for bands that were just me and a cousin in a garage. Fast forward to high school and I was the person everyone came to for posters, t-shirts, and the yearbook spread that "looked professional." I didn't have the language for it yet but I was already thinking about the user, even if the user was just the student body of Lincoln High.

College is where everything clicked. I bounced around a little — communications, then marketing, then a graphic design minor — because I was curious about everything and I didn't want to put myself in a box. One night I was in the library avoiding a statistics problem set and I fell down a rabbit hole about "design thinking" and Steve Jobs and how the best products in the world are the ones that feel invisible. I remember closing my laptop and thinking, that's it. That's what I want to do with my life. I want to craft experiences that feel so intuitive people don't even notice the design, they just feel delighted.

I took an online UX certificate (shoutout to my instructors, you know who you are!!) and built my first wireframes. They weren't perfect but they were mine. From there I started saying yes to everything: a landing page for a roommate's startup that never launched, a redesign concept of an app I use every day, a logo for my cousin's bakery Rise & Thyme which is actually doing really well now if you're ever in the East Side, please go, the focaccia is insane. Each project taught me something about myself as a designer and as a human.

Today I'm a passionate, empathetic, user-centric creative who believes design can change the world. I love collaborating, I love brainstorming, and I love that moment when a stakeholder looks at a mockup and goes "wait, that's exactly what I was picturing." I'm a people person through and through. I get my energy from workshops, from hallway conversations, from sitting with someone and really listening. Also from coffee. I will not pretend otherwise. Oat milk latte, extra shot, a little honey if they have it.

When I'm not designing you can usually find me hanging with my two cats, Mochi and Pixel (yes, Pixel is named after the thing, I'm not even sorry), rewatching Studio Ghibli movies, or wandering a farmers market pretending I'm going to cook something ambitious. I'm a Libra sun, Gemini moon, and I think that tracks. I believe in kindness, in details, and in the idea that every screen is a chance to make someone's day a tiny bit easier. If that sounds like someone you want on your team, I'd love to chat.
