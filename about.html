About

Product designer. Currently independent.

I’ve been designing digital products for about eight years, mostly in finance, health, and the public sector. I like problems that look operational from the outside and turn out to be about trust once you sit with them.

Before working independently I was at a product studio and, earlier, on an in-house team at a bank. I work closely with research, engineering, and whoever is actually going to have to live with the thing.

How I work
