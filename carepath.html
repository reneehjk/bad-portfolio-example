CarePath

Product design, healthcare · 2023

CarePath is a patient intake product used by a regional clinic network. I was brought in to help with the onboarding experience — the stretch from account creation through insurance and medical history, before a first appointment. The existing flow worked in the sense that people eventually got through it, but staff were spending a lot of time on incomplete records and follow-up calls. I joined for a four-month engagement alongside two engineers and a clinical operations lead.

Most of the visible work was simplifying the sequence of forms and making it clearer what could wait until later. I also spent time on the staff-facing view of a partially completed intake, which turned out to matter as much as the patient side. We used the clinic’s existing visual language rather than introducing a new one.

Process

  • Shadowing front-desk staff
  • Journey mapping
  • Sketching and task flows
  • High-fidelity UI in Figma
  • Usability sessions
  • Design QA in staging

Outcome

Intake completion improved and staff reported fewer follow-up calls. The clinic has since rolled the flow out to additional locations. Increased engagement with the pre-visit checklist was a nice side effect. I’m not able to share detailed metrics.
